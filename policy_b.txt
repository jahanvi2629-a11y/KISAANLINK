Government Circular No. 22/2024 - Amendment to Farmer Support Scheme

Applications must be submitted within 45 days of the notification date.
All beneficiaries are required to submit valid identity proof along with address verification.
The subsidy amount shall be Rs. 7000 per eligible household.
This scheme is optional for all registered farmers in the district.
Beneficiaries can withdraw the subsidy amount through any nationalized bank branch.
The scheme will remain valid for a period of 2 years from the date of issue.
