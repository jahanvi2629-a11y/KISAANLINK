Government Circular No. 14/2024 - Farmer Support Scheme

Applications must be submitted within 30 days of the notification date.
All beneficiaries are required to submit valid identity proof.
The subsidy amount shall be Rs. 5000 per eligible household.
This scheme is mandatory for all registered farmers in the district.
Beneficiaries can withdraw the subsidy amount only through the district cooperative bank.
The scheme will remain valid for a period of 2 years from the date of issue.
